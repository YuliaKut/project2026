🎨 Portrait of Ignazio Fumagalli – Project Documentation

1. Portrait Overview

Karl Brjullov – Portrait of Ignazio Fumagalli

The Portrait of Ignazio Fumagalli is a painting by the Russian artist Karl Bryullov (1799–1852), whose name is also transliterated as Brjullov or Briullov.

Bryullov is regarded as one of the most important Russian painters of the first half of the nineteenth century and a key figure in the transition from Russian neoclassicism to romanticism. His best-known work, The Last Day of Pompeii, established Bryullov as one of the finest European painters of his day. Beyond his monumental history paintings, much of his lasting reputation rests on his portraits, in which he combined romantic expressiveness with sharp psychological insight. He created this portrait during the years he spent working in Italy.

The sitter, Ignazio Fumagalli (1778–1842), was an Italian painter and engraver. He was born in Milan, studied at the Accademia di Brera, and served as its Secretary until his death. He also held the Chair of Aesthetics and worked as an art critic for the literary magazine Biblioteca Italiana. As a long-serving and influential figure at the Brera Academy, he was a natural and prestigious subject for a portrait.

The painting is kept in Milan, in the collection of the Accademia di Belle Arti di Brera (the Brera Academy), housed within the same complex as the famous Pinacoteca di Brera.

⚠️ Important Note: This isn't an "enrichment," but a correction to an existing value. Simply adding triplets (as with dimensions) won't do. If you simply append the correct string to Turtle, the column will end up with two descriptions—the old one with the typo, and the new one. Therefore, a SPARQL Update DELETE/INSERT operation is needed.
