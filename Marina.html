🏛️ Marina — Il Tesoro dei Medici

Moscow, Russia — The Armoury Chamber of the Kremlin Museums

“Il Tesoro dei Medici” was an exhibition held in Moscow in 2011. It ran from 20 May to 1 August 2011 and was organized as part of the cultural exchanges between Italy and Russia.

The exhibition presented the extraordinary decorative-art collection of the Medici family, the dynasty that ruled Florence and Tuscany during the Renaissance. The works came mainly from the Medici collections preserved in Florence.

The show focused not on paintings, but on the Medici passion for luxury objects and collecting, including jewellery, gold and silver works, and artistic objects that demonstrated the family’s wealth, taste, and political power.

A major theme of the exhibition was how the Medici used collecting as a way to express Renaissance ideals of culture, magnificence, and authority. The treasures reflected the tastes of rulers such as Cosimo I de' Medici and later members of the dynasty, including the final Medici heir Anna Maria Luisa de' Medici.

SPARQL Queries & Results

The following queries were used to explore the ArCo knowledge graph and retrieve information about cultural events in Russia.

Query 1: Events in Moscow

PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> PREFIX clv: <https://w3id.org/italia/onto/CLV/> PREFIX cis: <http://dati.beniculturali.it/cis/> SELECT * WHERE { ?place a cis:GeographicalFeature ; rdfs:label "Mosca" . ?event a cis:CulturalEvent ; clv:hasSpatialCoverage ?place ; rdfs:label ?eventLabel } ORDER BY DESC(?eventLabel) LIMIT 100

Description: Since we decided to work on events combining Russian and Italian cultural heritage, the initial first step is choosing an event. The main cities to consider in Russia are Moscow and Saint-Petersburg. The query for Saint-Petersburg (rdfs:label "Pietroburgo") has shown only one event held in 1902, while the query for Moscow (rdfs:label "Mosca") has shown much more options. My choice was "Il Tesoro dei Medici", 2011, since a) it is a modern event and it is easier to find relevant information about it; b) it has rich documentation available.

Query 2: Describe Event IRI

PREFIX cis: <https://w3id.org/arco/resource/CulturalEvent/> DESCRIBE cis:4c88dc5e0662f98f9c0ff26a0b51e4d3

Description: The first thing needed to be done is to determine the information about my event that is already present in the ArCo. Exploring the KG's ontology, we are able to identify and use classes and properties that can be used to model a needed query.

Information found in ArCo:

  • Name of the event (rdfs:label OR l0:name)
  • Type of the event (rdf:type)
  • Cultural entities involved (cis:involvesCulturalEntity)
  • Date of the event (ti:time)

❌ Missing information:

  • Specific museum venue (clv:hasSpatialCoverage) — EMPTY
  • Event organisers (arco:hasEventOrganiser) — EMPTY

Query 3: All Properties of the Event

PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#> SELECT ?property ?value ?valueLabel WHERE { <https://w3id.org/arco/resource/CulturalEvent/4c88dc5e0662f98f9c0ff26a0b51e4d3> ?property ?value . OPTIONAL { ?value rdfs:label ?valueLabel . } }

Result: The SPARQL query confirms that ArCo misses the specific place of the event and its organisers. These are the problems we are focusing on.

Using AI Tools to Enrich the Knowledge Graph

Step 1: Questions

Prompt for the LLMs:
"Provide me with necessary information on 'Il Tesoro dei Medici'. Provide me with the answer as following: 1) the date of the event; 2) the place of the event – country, city, museum; 3) the organisers of the event. The date of the event is being asked to avoid the possibility of events with the same name appearing."

Step 2: Answers

🤖 ChatGPT

Despite the format prompting, the LLM has given a lot of context on the event, thus "opening a dialogue" with the user. However, the tool managed to provide necessary information, immediately indicating reliable sources.

  • ✅ Date: 20 May – 1 August 2011
  • ✅ Place: Moscow, Armoury Chamber
  • ✅ Organisers: Gallerie degli Uffizi

🟣 Pi AI

The tool has not proven to be "the first emotionally intelligent AI" since it lacks the hooks to encourage communication with the user. What is even more important is the fact of Pi AI providing false information about the event, not considering the possibility of several events with the same name existing.

  • ❌ Provided incorrect dates
  • ❌ Wrong venue information

Conclusion: ChatGPT, although adding too much context to its replies, is a more reliable LLM and will be used for this project. For less reliable AI tools, more detailed engineering of the initial prompt is required.

Enriching ArCo Ontology with New Triples

Triple A: Spatial Coverage (Museum)

Solving problem a) what museum held my event in Moscow.
Combining the ArCo ontology fully provided in its dictionaries and LLMs, we design new triplets, thus enriching the ArCo ontology.
Answer known: the event occurred in Russia, Moscow, in the Armoury Chamber of the Kremlin Museums.
New triplet: Il Tesoro dei Medici – hasSpatialCoverage – the Kremlin Museums.

Triple B: Event Organiser

https://w3id.org/arco/resource/CulturalInstituteOrSite/DBunicoCG20403 event:isEventOrganiserof https://w3id.org/arco/resource/CulturalEvent/4c88dc5e0662f98f9c0ff26a0b51e4d3

Solving problem b) who has organised my event.
Answer known: the event was organised by Gallerie degli Uffizi, Florence.
New triplet (option 1): Gallerie degli Uffizi – isEventOrganiserof – Il Tesoro dei Medici
OR
New triplet (option 2): Il Tesoro dei Medici – hasEventOrganiser – Gallerie degli Uffizi
